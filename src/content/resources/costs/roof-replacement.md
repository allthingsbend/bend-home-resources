## The biggest pricing variables

- Roof area and pitch.
- Number of stories and access difficulty.
- Existing roofing layers and tear-off requirements.
- Decking damage discovered after removal.
- Roofing material and product line.
- Flashing, valleys, skylights and penetrations.
- Ventilation changes.
- Disposal, permits and site protection.

## How to request comparable bids

Give each roofer the same known information and ask for the same breakdown. Require proposals to state material, tear-off, underlayment, flashing, ventilation, damaged-decking allowance, permit responsibility and warranty.

If one bid is dramatically lower, identify what is missing before deciding it is a better value.

## Budget for uncertainty

Decking and concealed water damage may not be visible until materials are removed. Ask how those conditions are priced and whether there is a unit price or allowance.

For a home purchase, distinguish between 'replace immediately,' 'repair and monitor,' and 'likely replacement within several years.' Those scenarios have very different financial implications.
