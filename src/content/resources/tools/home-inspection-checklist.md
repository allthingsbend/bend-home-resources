## Exterior and water

- Roof age/condition and visible repairs.
- Flashing, penetrations and roof drainage.
- Gutters/downspouts and discharge locations.
- Siding, trim, soffits and fascia.
- Grading and hardscape slope.
- Decks, railings and exterior structures.
- Retaining walls and visible movement.
- Window/door exterior condition.

## Interior and structure

- Ceiling/wall staining or moisture signs.
- Floors that are noticeably uneven or damaged.
- Foundation/crawlspace observations.
- Attic access, insulation and ventilation.
- Evidence of prior structural repairs.
- Areas the inspector cannot access.

## Systems

- Electrical service/panel and visible concerns.
- Plumbing supply/drain observations.
- Water heater.
- Heating and cooling equipment.
- Fireplace/wood-stove questions where applicable.
- Smoke/CO safety devices.

## Specialist follow-up

- Sewer scope.
- Roofer.
- Electrician.
- Plumber.
- HVAC contractor.
- Structural engineer.
- Moisture/mold professional.
- General contractor for repair scoping.

## Three questions for the inspector

1. What are the three to five findings you would prioritize if you were buying this house?
2. What could you not inspect or access?
3. Which findings truly need specialist evaluation during the contingency period?
