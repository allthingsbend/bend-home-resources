## When to call a roofer

- The home inspector sees active leakage or damaged materials.
- Roof age is unknown and remaining life matters to the purchase decision.
- There are multiple layers or signs of prior patching.
- Flashing, penetrations or valleys need specialist review.
- An insurer asks for roof information before binding coverage.

## What a useful roof quote should say

Ask what roofing system is being proposed, what tear-off is included, how damaged decking is handled, the underlayment and flashing approach, ventilation work, disposal, permit responsibility and warranty.

If the roof does not need replacement yet, ask for repair and maintenance options rather than assuming replacement is the only answer.

## Compare contractor credentials and scope

Use Oregon CCB resources to verify licensing. For a major roof project, request written proposals detailed enough that you can see why totals differ.

## Think beyond shingles

A roof works with gutters, flashing, attic ventilation, siding transitions and drainage. If water problems exist, replacing the roof alone may not address the real pathway.
