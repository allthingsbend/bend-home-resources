## Get equivalent scenarios

When you compare lenders, ask each to quote the same purchase price, down payment, loan type and lock assumptions. Otherwise a lower rate may simply reflect more points or a different scenario.

Once formal estimates are available, compare fees and cash-to-close line by line.

## Questions to ask

- What rate and APR apply to this exact scenario?
- Are points or lender credits included?
- Which lender fees are charged?
- How long is the rate lock and what happens if closing moves?
- What are the underwriting turn times?
- Who will be my day-to-day contact after application?
- Can you show scenarios with different down payments?

## Local versus national

A local lender may know Bend-area workflows and be easier to reach during a transaction. A larger lender may have different pricing or products. Neither model is automatically better; compare service and total loan economics.

## Verify licensing where applicable

Oregon's Division of Financial Regulation oversees mortgage industry licensing, and NMLS Consumer Access can be used for many nonbank lender and loan-originator checks.
