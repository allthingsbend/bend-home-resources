## Understand representation before you sign

Oregon requires written buyer representation agreements in circumstances where a broker or principal broker assists a buyer in searching for or purchasing real estate. The agreement should make responsibilities, expectations and compensation clearer.

Read the term, geographic or property scope, compensation, exclusivity and cancellation provisions. Ask for explanations in plain English before signing.

## What local knowledge actually matters

- How different Bend neighborhoods and housing ages affect due diligence.
- Common HOA, access, irrigation or property-record questions.
- Local inspection and specialist availability.
- How wildfire, drainage, snow and exterior maintenance can affect a property.
- Experience coordinating complex repair estimates during inspection periods.

## Questions to ask an agent

1. How do you help buyers evaluate a house beyond comparable sales?
2. What is your process when an inspection finds something significant?
3. How do you handle conflicts or disclosed limited agency situations?
4. How is your compensation structured and when could I owe anything directly?
5. How quickly do you communicate during an active negotiation?
6. Can you explain the buyer representation agreement and how cancellation works?

## Red flags

- Pressure to sign documents you do not understand.
- Dismissing inspection concerns without evidence or specialist input.
- Vague answers about compensation.
- Poor responsiveness when deadlines matter.
- Treating every property concern as 'normal' without helping you investigate it.
