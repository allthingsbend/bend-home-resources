## Primary sources first

For laws, licensing, permits, public records and safety guidance, we prefer official state, county and City of Bend sources. Secondary sources can add context but should not override the primary authority.

## No fake local data

We will not publish a precise 'Bend average' simply because a national calculator can generate one. Cost pages will distinguish sourced estimates from our own collected local quote data and will describe methodology.

## Directories are not endorsements

A business appearing on a future professional page will not automatically mean Bend Home Resources endorses it. We intend to disclose selection criteria, sponsorship and affiliate relationships where relevant.

## Clear update dates

High-change pages should show when they were reviewed. When regulations or program details matter, readers should be able to reach the current official source.

## Corrections

If we find a material error, the priority is to correct the page. Readers and local professionals will be able to contact us with factual corrections once the site's contact workflow is enabled.
