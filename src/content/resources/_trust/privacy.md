## Analytics

The site is built to support optional Google Analytics configuration. If analytics is enabled, the service may collect technical and usage information according to its own terms and the site's final consent/configuration choices.

## Forms and contact information

The launch build does not require a public contact form. If forms are added later, this policy should be updated to explain what information is collected, why it is used and how long it is retained.

## Third-party links

Pages may link to government agencies, professional licensing resources and other third-party sites. Their privacy practices are governed by their own policies.

## Updates

This policy should be updated as the site's data practices change.
