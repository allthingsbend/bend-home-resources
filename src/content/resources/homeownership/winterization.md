## Protect water systems

- Follow the correct winterization procedure for your irrigation system.
- Disconnect hoses and address vulnerable exterior fixtures.
- Know the location of the main water shutoff.
- Insulate or protect exposed piping where appropriate.
- Fix active exterior leaks before freezing weather.

## Service the heating system

Schedule routine maintenance appropriate to your system and replace filters where applicable. If the system has been unreliable, handle diagnosis before the first major cold stretch rather than during it.

Know what backup heat or emergency plan is safe for your home.

## Handle roof and drainage

Clean gutters and downspouts, check discharge paths and inspect visible roof concerns. Water that cannot drain properly can create problems once temperatures cycle above and below freezing.

Address obvious grading or pooling problems before frozen ground makes exterior work harder.

## Seal obvious air and pest pathways

Check weatherstripping, exterior penetrations and accessible gaps. Air-sealing work should be compatible with safe ventilation and combustion requirements for the home.

## Create a simple freeze plan

If you travel, decide who can access the house if a problem occurs. Keep key contractor contacts, shutoff information and insurance details somewhere accessible.
