## Start closest to the structure

Local guidance encourages homeowners to pay particular attention to the first feet around the home. Remove accumulated needles, leaves and combustible debris from places where embers could collect.

Look at decks, roof valleys, gutters, foundation edges and items stored directly against siding.

## Manage vegetation and trees

Bend Fire & Rescue publishes practical spacing and maintenance recommendations, including keeping grass short and managing tree branches around roofs and power lines.

Do not treat defensible space as a one-time clearing project. Vegetation grows back and seasonal debris returns.

## Make access part of preparedness

Keep the address visible and think about whether first responders can access the property. Store emergency information and understand your household's evacuation plan.

## Buying a home? Inspect wildfire maintenance too

During a showing or inspection, notice roof/gutter debris, dense vegetation against structures, wood piles, combustible fencing or landscaping and the overall maintenance burden. These are ownership considerations even when they are not defects in the traditional inspection sense.

## Use the local source, not a generic checklist

Requirements and recommendations can evolve. Bend Home Resources links to Bend Fire & Rescue so readers can check the current local guidance rather than relying solely on a static article.
