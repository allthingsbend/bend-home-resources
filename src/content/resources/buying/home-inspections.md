## What a general inspection is good at

A standard inspection typically evaluates visible and accessible components of the structure and major systems. The exact scope depends on the inspector and standards of practice, so ask what is included before booking.

In Oregon, individuals who inspect residential structures for a fee and provide a written report are subject to state home-inspector certification requirements. Verify credentials rather than relying only on reviews.

## Bend-specific areas to pay attention to

- Roof condition, flashing, penetrations and evidence of prior leaks.
- Gutters, grading and whether water is directed away from the structure.
- Exterior siding, trim, decks and exposed wood.
- Crawlspace or basement moisture where applicable.
- Freeze-related plumbing vulnerabilities and exterior hose bibs.
- Heating system type, age and service history.
- Electrical panels and updates in older homes.
- Wildfire-related vegetation and exterior vulnerabilities.
- Visible signs of unpermitted or amateur remodel work.

## When to bring in a specialist

A general inspector may recommend further evaluation by a roofer, electrician, plumber, HVAC contractor, engineer, sewer-scope provider, mold professional or other specialist. Prioritize recommendations based on severity, uncertainty and potential cost.

If a finding could change whether you buy the house or materially change the economics, try to investigate it during the contingency period rather than guessing.

## How to triage the report

1. Life-safety and immediate hazards.
2. Active water intrusion or moisture risk.
3. Structural concerns.
4. Major systems near failure or requiring specialist review.
5. Exterior-envelope and drainage problems.
6. Deferred maintenance.
7. Cosmetic or optional improvements.

## Attend the end if you can

Even if you cannot attend the full inspection, meeting the inspector near the end can be useful. Ask for the three to five items they would focus on first, what they could not access, and which recommendations truly need specialist follow-up.
